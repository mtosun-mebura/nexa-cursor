# NEXA Taxi – Concept Algemene Voorwaarden Platform, Netwerk en Chauffeurs

> **Concept voor juridische review.** Dit document is een product/engineering draft and is not a substitute for review by a Dutch lawyer, tax adviser and payment/compliance specialist before publication. Company details, addresses, KvK/VAT numbers, governing entity and exact fee/settlement periods must be completed before use.

## 1. Definitions
**NEXA**: the operator of the NEXA Suite/NEXA Taxi technology platform.  
**Platform**: NEXA Suite, NEXA Taxi, websites, apps, APIs and related services.  
**Customer**: a person or organization requesting transport.  
**Taxi Company**: an independent transport provider using the Platform.  
**Driver**: a natural person performing transport for/through an approved Taxi Company or, where legally and contractually permitted, as an approved independent provider.  
**Booking Owner**: the Taxi Company with which the Customer's booking relationship is established.  
**Fulfilling Company**: the Taxi Company actually performing a ride.  
**NEXA Marketplace**: bookings generated through NEXA's general marketplace.  
**NEXA Network**: optional capacity network through which a Booking Owner can offer a ride to other approved providers.  
**Payment Provider**: the licensed third party used for payment processing, verification, routing and/or settlement.

## 2. Role of NEXA
NEXA provides technology for booking, dispatch, communication, tracking, payments and optional matching between Customers and transport providers. Unless expressly stated otherwise for a specific transaction, the Taxi Company/Fulfilling Company is responsible for performing the transport and for complying with applicable transport, tax, employment, licensing and insurance obligations.

NEXA does not guarantee that a driver or vehicle will always be available.

## 3. Account registration
Users must provide accurate, current information and keep account credentials confidential. Accounts and permissions are personal and may not be transferred without permission.

Selecting “Driver” during registration does not create an unconditional right to perform rides. A Driver may remain pending until required identity, company affiliation, credentials, vehicle, payment/KYB and other checks have been completed.

A Taxi Company may remain pending until business and authorized-representative checks are complete.

NEXA may require email/phone verification, stronger authentication, additional documents or renewed verification where reasonably necessary for security, compliance or payments.

## 4. Customer accounts
Customers must provide correct booking information and a valid payment method where required. The Customer must not abuse booking, cancellation, refund, complaint, rating or promotional functionality.

Prices, cancellation conditions and the identity of the contracting/performing provider must be shown as clearly as reasonably possible before or during the booking flow.

## 5. Driver obligations
A Driver must:
- only perform rides while legally entitled and operationally fit to do so;
- use only an approved account and, where required, approved vehicle;
- not share the driver account;
- keep required credentials and company affiliation current;
- start, update and complete rides truthfully;
- not manipulate GPS, timestamps, distance, duration, fare, passenger state, payment status or device identity;
- not create sham bookings or collude with Customers, companies or other Drivers to generate payments;
- immediately report material errors, safety incidents and unauthorized account use.

## 6. Taxi Company obligations
A Taxi Company is responsible for its drivers, vehicles, permits, insurance, tariffs, consumer information, tax obligations and other legal requirements applicable to its transport business, except to the extent NEXA expressly assumes a responsibility in writing.

A Taxi Company must not use the Network to circumvent legal, licensing, employment, insurance or tax requirements.

## 7. NEXA Network
Participation in the NEXA Network is optional unless otherwise expressly agreed.

Where a Booking Owner offers a ride to the Network, another approved Taxi Company may become the Fulfilling Company. The original Booking Owner does not automatically lose its customer relationship solely because another provider performs the ride.

The Fulfilling Company receives only information reasonably necessary to assess and perform the offered ride, subject to applicable privacy rules.

## 8. Marketplace
A booking originating through the general NEXA Marketplace may be allocated/offered to eligible participating providers according to the Platform's dispatch and availability rules.

Marketplace fees and Network fees are separate concepts and may have different rates.

## 9. Prices and fees
Applicable subscription, marketplace, network, transaction or other fees are shown in the agreement, dashboard, pricing page or booking/offer flow as applicable.

NEXA may change future fees with appropriate notice where required. A completed ride's financial records should use the fee/rule snapshot applicable to that transaction rather than later pricing.

## 10. Payments and payout onboarding
Payments and settlements may be processed by a licensed Payment Provider. Users may be required to accept that provider's terms and complete identity/business/payment-account verification.

NEXA may store provider identifiers and payment status information, but payment credentials may be held and verified by the Payment Provider.

A Driver or Taxi Company has no right to payment merely because a “ride completed” button was pressed.

## 11. Verification of completed rides
A ride may become payable only after NEXA and/or the Payment Provider has sufficient evidence that the ride was legitimately performed.

NEXA may use proportionate fraud and integrity signals, including:
- server-side ride states and timestamps;
- payment status;
- pickup/start/completion events;
- route, location, distance and duration plausibility;
- cancellation/refund/dispute state;
- overlapping rides;
- Customer confirmation or complaint;
- unusual account, device, booking or payout patterns;
- recent payout-destination changes.

Automated signals may result in a temporary hold or additional review. A single signal does not necessarily establish fraud.

## 12. Holds, reviews and settlements
NEXA may delay settlement where reasonably necessary to verify a ride, investigate anomalies, process a complaint/refund/chargeback, comply with law/payment-provider requirements, or protect Customers/providers/the Platform against fraud or financial loss.

Where practical, the affected user will be informed of a hold and may be asked for information.

Funds are released according to the applicable settlement rules after the transaction becomes eligible.

## 13. Fraud and misuse
Prohibited conduct includes:
- fake or staged rides;
- falsely marking pickup, passenger-on-board or completion;
- GPS/location manipulation;
- creating accounts to evade suspension or limits;
- identity/document/payment fraud;
- unauthorized use of another person's/company's account;
- manipulating fares, refunds, promotions, ratings or payments;
- collusion intended to generate unjustified payouts;
- technical attacks, scraping beyond permitted use, vulnerability exploitation or bypassing authorization.

NEXA may restrict or suspend functionality while credible abuse is investigated. Permanent termination or recovery of unjustified amounts may follow where supported by the facts, contract and applicable law.

## 14. Refunds, chargebacks and corrections
Refunds, chargebacks and financial corrections may affect amounts payable to the relevant provider. Where an amount has already been settled, a corresponding correction may be offset against future amounts or otherwise recovered where legally and contractually permitted.

Financial history should remain auditable; corrections do not erase the original transaction.

## 15. Payout destination changes
Changes to a payout destination may require renewed authentication, MFA, provider verification and a security waiting period. NEXA may temporarily block settlement to a newly changed destination where reasonably necessary to reduce account-takeover fraud.

## 16. Suspension and termination
NEXA may temporarily restrict an account or feature for security, compliance, payment-provider, legal or credible fraud-risk reasons. Where appropriate, NEXA will provide information about the reason and available review process, except where doing so would undermine security/investigation or is legally restricted.

Users may terminate their account subject to outstanding rides, payments, disputes, retention duties and contractual notice periods.

## 17. Privacy and data
Personal data is processed according to the applicable privacy notice. NEXA aims to apply data minimization, purpose limitation, access control, retention rules and privacy by design/default.

For Network rides, data sharing between independent companies must be limited to what is necessary for offering and performing the ride or meeting legal obligations.

## 18. Location and ride data
Drivers acknowledge that location and ride-event data may be processed while using operational ride functionality for dispatch, safety, trip execution, dispute handling and fraud prevention, subject to the privacy notice and applicable law.

Location data is not infallible and may be combined with other evidence.

## 19. Platform reporting and tax information
Where NEXA qualifies for a statutory platform-reporting obligation, including potentially DAC7, NEXA may collect, verify, retain and report information required by law and may provide affected sellers/service providers with required summaries.

Each provider remains responsible for its own tax position unless the law provides otherwise.

## 20. Intellectual property and acceptable use
The Platform, software, branding and documentation remain protected by applicable intellectual-property rights. Users receive only the rights necessary to use the service under their agreement.

Reverse engineering, security circumvention and unauthorized access are prohibited except to the extent mandatory law expressly permits otherwise.

## 21. Availability and changes
NEXA aims to provide a reliable service but cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability. Maintenance, third-party outages, connectivity, GPS, map, payment and device services can affect operation.

Material changes to the service or terms will be communicated as required by the applicable agreement and law.

## 22. Liability
Liability limitations must be completed and reviewed by Dutch counsel before publication. They must not exclude liability where exclusion is prohibited by mandatory law, including applicable consumer protections.

Do not publish a generic “NEXA is never liable” clause.

## 23. Complaints and disputes
Customers, Drivers and Taxi Companies must have an accessible channel to report booking, payment, safety, privacy and account issues. NEXA may request supporting information and preserve relevant audit records.

The final production terms must specify the legal entity, contact details, applicable complaint process, governing law and competent forum, subject to mandatory consumer rules.

## 24. Version and acceptance
The Platform must record the accepted Terms version, timestamp and account identity. For material changes, renewed acceptance may be required.
